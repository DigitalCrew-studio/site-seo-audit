### Anti-rules added

- Do not treat `llms.txt` as mandatory for Google Search.
- Do not require every social platform.
- Do not treat Facebook Pixel as a core SEO issue.
- Do not invent backlink data.
- Do not score unassessed categories as if they were verified.
- Do not treat SPF/DMARC or social profiles as direct ranking factors.
